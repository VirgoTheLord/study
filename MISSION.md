# Mission: ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (Computer Science) written test

## Why
The user sits the ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' Computer Science written test tomorrow (Part A: 80 discipline questions, Part B: 15 aptitude questions) and wants to learn every topic that appeared in the 2023 and 2025 papers from scratch, as if they had forgotten or never learned it, and then answer questions that repeat those topics.

## Success looks like
- Recognises every topic that appeared in the 2023 and 2025 papers and can answer the standard question shapes for it
- Can do the numeric patterns fast: window size/BDP, subnetting, Banker's, page replacement, FCFS, RSA, function points, cyclomatic complexity
- Can eliminate wrong options on theory questions (closure properties, normal forms, OSI layers, security terms)

## Constraints
- Learn at their own pace; the 5-hour plan was dropped on request
- Beginner level: explain ideas from first principles, assume little or forgotten prior knowledge
- Every topic ends with a past-paper question (PYQ) in the style it is asked
- Source material: the 2023 and 2025 ISRO CS papers in this folder

## Out of scope
- Formal proofs
- Topics that never appeared in the two papers, unless they sit right next to one that did
